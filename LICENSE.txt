PowerPotion is a clean-room, personalized companion for Autodesk Revit that reuses the
rendering engine of your separately installed Enscape product at runtime, through the
managed API assembly that Enscape itself ships (Enscape.RendererApi.dll).

1. What this software does
   • Adds a "PowerPotion" ribbon tab to Revit 2026 with real-time rendering,
     material editing, sun/location, sky/cloud/fog, quality and capture controls.
   • Loads the native Enscape engine from your licensed Enscape installation and
     embeds its render window in its own host process.
   • Contains no VR, account, telemetry or license-activation functionality.

2. What this software does NOT do
   • It does not modify, patch, decode, redistribute or bypass any Chaos/Enscape
     binary, protection or entitlement mechanism. Any license enforcement that the
     Enscape engine performs internally remains fully in effect.
   • It does not include any Chaos-owned code or assets.

3. Requirements on your part
   • A valid, separately obtained Enscape license. PowerPotion is a companion UI,
     not a replacement for one. If your Enscape installation is unlicensed or its
     trial has ended, the engine may refuse to render — by design.
   • Autodesk Revit 2026, licensed separately.
   • Use within the scope of the licenses you hold for Revit and Enscape.
