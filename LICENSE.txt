PowerPotion is a clean-room, personalized companion for Autodesk Revit. It ships its own
standalone Vulkan 1.3 renderer and does not load, link to, modify or redistribute any
Chaos/Enscape binary, shader, texture or other asset.

1. What this software does
   • Adds a "PowerPotion" ribbon tab to Revit 2026 with real-time rendering,
     material editing, sun/location, sky/cloud/fog, quality and capture controls.
   • Renders with PowerPotion's own Vulkan renderer in its own host process.
   • Contains no VR, account, telemetry or license-activation functionality.

2. What this software does NOT do
   • It does not include, copy or modify any Chaos-owned code or assets.
   • It does not require or use an Enscape installation.

3. Requirements on your part
   • Autodesk Revit 2026, licensed separately.
   • Windows 10/11 x64 with a Vulkan 1.3-capable GPU.
   • Use within the scope of the licenses you hold for Revit and any other tools you use.
