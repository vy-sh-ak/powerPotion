PowerPotion is a clean-room, personalized companion for Autodesk Revit that reuses the
rendering engine of your separately installed renderer product at runtime, through the
managed API assembly that the engine product itself ships (RendererApi assembly).

1. What this software does
   • Adds a "PowerPotion" ribbon tab to Revit 2026 with real-time rendering,
     material editing, sun/location, sky/cloud/fog, quality and capture controls.
   • Loads the native rendering engine from your licensed renderer installation and
     embeds its render window in its own host process.
   • Contains no VR, account, telemetry or license-activation functionality.

2. What this software does NOT do
   • It does not modify, patch, decode, redistribute or bypass any third-party
     binary, protection or entitlement mechanism. Any license enforcement that the
     rendering engine performs internally remains fully in effect.
   • It does not include any third-party-owned code or assets.

3. Requirements on your part
   • A valid, separately obtained license for the rendering engine product.
     PowerPotion is a companion UI, not a replacement for one. If your engine
     installation is unlicensed or its trial has ended, the engine may refuse
     to render — by design.
   • Autodesk Revit 2026, licensed separately.
   • Use within the scope of the licenses you hold for Revit and the engine product.
